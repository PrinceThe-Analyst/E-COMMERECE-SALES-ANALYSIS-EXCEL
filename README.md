# 📊 E-Commerce Store Analytics (Excel)

## 📖 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end analysis of an e-commerce sales dataset using Microsoft Excel. The objective was to clean, prepare, and analyze sales data to uncover meaningful business insights that support data-driven decision-making.

The project explores revenue performance, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, platform comparison, and geographic sales trends using Excel Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

This project answers the following business questions:

- How many transactions were completed?
- How much revenue did the business generate?
- What is the average item price?
- What is the average quantity purchased per order?
- What is the average customer rating?
- How does revenue change across different months?
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which product categories perform the best?
- Which brands contribute the most revenue?
- Are there differences in performance across platforms?
- Which cities generate the highest revenue?
- Are there observable monthly sales trends?

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

The following preprocessing steps were performed before analysis:

- Retrieved and inspected the dataset.
- Checked for duplicate records.
- Confirmed that no duplicate records were found.
- Formatted the **Price** column using Accounting format.
- Formatted the **Total Amount** column using Accounting format.
- Renamed the **Reviews** column to **Review Count** for better clarity.
- Converted the **Order Date** column to the appropriate Date format.
- Created an **Order Month** column for monthly trend analysis.

## 🔍 Key Findings

- The business generated approximately **₦302 million** in revenue from **10,000 transactions**.
- Electronics generated the highest revenue among all product categories, followed closely by Fashion.
- Xiaomi Redmi was the highest revenue-generating product.
- Souq slightly outperformed Jumia and Amazon in total revenue, although platform performance was relatively balanced.
- Cairo generated the highest revenue, followed by Casablanca and Riyadh.
- July, September, January, and May recorded the highest monthly sales.
- Customers purchased an average of **3 items per transaction**.
- The average customer rating was **3.01 out of 5**, indicating moderate customer satisfaction.

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

### Pricing Strategy

Maintain competitive pricing for high-performing categories such as Electronics and Fashion while introducing discounts or promotional bundles for lower-performing categories like Accessories.

### Inventory Management

Increase inventory for top-selling products such as Xiaomi Redmi, Smartwatch Fit, and Nike Air Max to avoid stock shortages and maximize revenue.

### Platform Optimization

Continue investing in Souq while improving marketing campaigns and promotional activities on Amazon to strengthen its performance.

### Geographic Marketing

Focus marketing efforts on high-performing cities such as Cairo, Casablanca, and Riyadh while implementing targeted campaigns to improve sales in lower-performing cities.

### Customer Experience

Improve customer satisfaction by collecting customer feedback, enhancing product quality, and strengthening customer support to increase future ratings and repeat purchases.

### Sales Planning

Use the high-performing months (January, May, July, and September) to better plan inventory, staffing, and promotional campaigns. Although the dataset contains only one year of data, these months represent sales peaks.
